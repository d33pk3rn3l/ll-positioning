Model, reference frame & interpretation

First-order Gaussian covariance model. Gravity-aligned roll and pitch. Initial heading uses the small-angle approximation. Motion variance grows linearly with distance walked. Starting-position, heading, initialization and motion errors remain separate.

Global frame: synthetic lander-centred east–north–up aligned to DE421 mean Earth. Up is geometric height above the synthetic tangent plane. No lunar terrain or frame transform is applied. Each plot shows one axis standard deviation; the ellipsoid’s principal semiaxes equal 1σ, without a confidence percentage.

UWB measures rover–lander distance. Orbital matching measures horizontal position relative to a map with one shared control offset. The joint covariance retains correlations between rover, heading, lander and map. Strong independent lander knowledge can reduce the map control limit. Both methods can be enabled together.

Thin possible trajectories are deterministic one-standard-deviation baseline perturbations, not posterior samples. In local view, nearby terrain and rocks move with the reference frame. Full formulas and research interpretation: model note ↗.
