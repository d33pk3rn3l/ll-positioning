# deutsch2019procellarum: GRAIL-identified gravity anomalies in Oceanus Procellarum: insight into subsurface impact and magmatic structures on the Moon (Deutsch, Neumann, Head, Wilson, Icarus 331:192-208, 2019)
Abbreviations: GRAIL = Gravity Recovery and Interior Laboratory; PBGA = positive Bouguer gravity anomaly; GRGM1200B = Goddard GRAIL gravity model to degree 1200.

## Claim
Forward models of four positive Bouguer anomalies in central Oceanus Procellarum, two of them in the Marius Hills (north and south). The paper uses 2800 kg/m^3 as the near-surface Bouguer reduction density for this mare region and tests 2500 kg/m^3 for the crust.

## Depth and region
Central Oceanus Procellarum including the Marius Hills. 2800 kg/m^3 is a near-surface reduction density at ~8 km resolution. 3090 kg/m^3 refers to the mare down to ~5 km (secondary).

## Application to a walking quadruped gravimeter on the Moon near the Marius Hills pit (LunarLeaper)
This is the closest GRAIL-era regional Bouguer density for the Marius Hills mare surface layer (2800 kg/m^3 used; 2750-2900 kg/m^3 cited). It is at ~8 km scale, so it does not resolve the regolith or the pit.

## Grade
C (model). Forward modeling of flight gravity data; the densities are adopted or cited, not solved for at the site.
