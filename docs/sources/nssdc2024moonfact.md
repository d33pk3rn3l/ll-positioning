# nssdc2024moonfact: NASA one-page table of Moon size, mass, gravity and orbit values (NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive Moon Fact Sheet, D. R. Williams, updated 11 January 2024)
Abbreviations in the title: NASA = National Aeronautics and Space Administration.

## Claim
Compiled bulk and orbital parameters of the Moon. A compilation, not a primary determination. Used here only to explain where 1.62 m/s^2 comes from.

## Numbers (with units and conditions)
Format: value | product | frame realization | statistic type. "not stated" = the source does not say.
- "Surface gravity (m/s^2) 1.62"; "Surface acceleration (m/s^2) 1.62" | compilation | n/a | not stated (3 significant figures).
- Definition (notes page): "Gravity - The gravitational acceleration on the surface at the equator ..., including the effects of rotation." | compilation.
- "GM (x 10^6 km^3/s^2) 0.00490" | compilation | not stated.
- "Volumetric mean radius (km) 1737.4"; "Equatorial radius (km) 1738.1"; "Polar radius (km) 1736.0" | compilation.
- "Revolution period (days) 27.3217" (sidereal); "Synodic period (days) 29.53"; "Sidereal rotation period (hrs) 655.720" | compilation | not stated.

## Height reference
Equator, radius not stated for g.

## Application to a walking quadruped on the Moon (LunarLeaper)
1.62 m/s^2 is a 3-figure rounded compiled value at the equator. For a sourced value use konopliv2013gl0660b gravitational constant times mass (GM) with the 1737.4 km sphere (1.6242 m/s^2 [derived]). The source of 1.625 used by the team is not known; I found no source that gives 1.625.
