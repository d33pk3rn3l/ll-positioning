# Report 2015 of the International Astronomical Union working group on cartographic coordinates and rotational elements (Archinal et al. 2018)

Citation key: `archinal2018wgccre`. Original title: "Report of the IAU Working Group on Cartographic Coordinates and Rotational Elements: 2015". Journal: Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy (CMDA). IAU = International Astronomical Union. WGCCRE = Working Group on Cartographic Coordinates and Rotational Elements.

## Claim
The 2015 WGCCRE report removes the low-precision series for the orientation of the Moon. For the Moon and Titan, it gives one reference radius for cartographic use and a different one for orthoprojection and geophysical use. It repeats the recommendation to make controlled cartographic products.
