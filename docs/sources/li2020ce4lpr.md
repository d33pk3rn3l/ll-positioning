# li2020ce4lpr: The Moon's farside shallow subsurface structure unveiled by Chang'E-4 LPR (Li et al. 2020, Sci. Adv. 6:eaay6898)

## Claim
Yutu-2 500 MHz LPR imaged ~40 m deep along a ~106 m route: low-loss granular ejecta with boulders.

## Numbers (with units and conditions)
Format: value | product | frame realization | statistic type. "not stated" = the source does not say.
- Frequencies: 60 MHz (band 40-80 MHz) and 500 MHz (band 250-750 MHz) | CE-4 LPR | n/a.
- Trace interval 0.6636 s (500 MHz), 1.536 s (60 MHz); rover ~5.5 cm/s -> approximate step ~3.6 cm (500 MHz), ~8.5 cm (60 MHz) | flight | approximate.
- Route used ~106 m (A to LE210); 13,400 (low) and 31,749 (high) traces over the first two lunar days | flight.
- Antennas ~0.3 m above ground, 0.16 m apart | design.
- Wave velocity v = 0.16 m/ns (from hyperbola fit, constant below ~70 ns) | assumption beyond.
- Loss tangent (5 ± 2) x 10^-3 | uncertainty type not stated.
- Lander 45.4446°S, 177.5991°E | frame not stated.
- How each trace was positioned (and its accuracy): not stated. Trace spacing is inferred from mean rover speed.

## Height reference
Depth from two-way time; no geodetic height reference.

## Application to a walking quadruped on the Moon (LunarLeaper)
Flight GPR reference: trace step ~3.6 cm from time-triggered sampling and mean speed. LunarLeaper plans 10 cm. For a walking robot with stop-and-go motion, time-triggered traces would be uneven; the position of each trace must come from navigation.
