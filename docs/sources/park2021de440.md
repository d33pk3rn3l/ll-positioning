# park2021de440: The JPL Planetary and Lunar Ephemerides DE440 and DE441 (Park et al. 2021, AJ 161:105)

## Claim
DE440 lunar mantle orientation is defined by the principal axes from GRAIL gravity analyses. Most cartographic products use the DE421 MER frame. The rotation from DE440 PA to DE421 MER is estimated by comparing the five retroreflector positions in both frames.

## Numbers (with units and conditions)
Format: value | product | frame realization | statistic type. "not stated" = the source does not say.
- DE440 PA -> DE421 MER: r_MER,DE421 = Rx(-0.2785) Ry(-78.6944) Rz(-67.8526) r_PA,DE440 | DE440 PA to DE421 MER | angles in arcsec (unit symbol lost in PDF text; NAIF kernel moon_de440_250416.tf gives the same numbers in ARCSECONDS) | uncertainty not stated in the parts I read.

- LLR rms residual ~20 cm (1970-1980 data), ~1.3 cm (recent data) | DE440 fit | one-way range | RMS.
- DE440 covers 1550-2650; DE441 -13,200 to +17,191 (less accurate this century) | n/a.

## Height reference
Cartesian XYZ from the centre of mass (m).

## Application to a walking quadruped on the Moon (LunarLeaper)
Gives the current ME realization used by NAIF (MOON_ME_DE440_ME421). New products should state 'DE440 PA' or 'DE421-aligned ME'. LunarLeaper nav and gravity products must carry this tag.
