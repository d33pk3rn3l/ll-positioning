# The DE440 and DE441 planetary and lunar ephemerides of the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (Park et al. 2021, Astronomical Journal 161:105)

Citation key: `park2021de440`. Original title: "The JPL Planetary and Lunar Ephemerides DE440 and DE441". JPL = Jet Propulsion Laboratory; DE = Development Ephemeris.

## Claim
In DE440, the principal axes (PA) from the gravity analyses of the Gravity Recovery and Interior Laboratory (GRAIL) define the orientation of the lunar mantle. Most cartographic products use the DE421 mean Earth / mean rotation (MER) frame. The authors estimate the rotation from DE440 PA to DE421 MER. They compare the positions of the five retroreflectors in both frames.

- DE440 covers 1550-2650; DE441 -13,200 to +17,191 (less accurate this century) | not applicable.

## Height reference
Cartesian XYZ from the centre of mass (m).

## Application to a walking quadruped on the Moon (LunarLeaper)
Gives the current mean Earth (ME) realization that NAIF uses: `MOON_ME_DE440_ME421`. New products must state 'DE440 PA' or 'DE421-aligned ME'. LunarLeaper navigation and gravity products must carry this tag. The source gives no uncertainty for the DE440 PA to DE421 ME rotation (see `williams2013de430` for the assumption label).
