# Requirements for gravity measurements on the planned Artemis III mission (James, Ermakov & Sori 2020, arXiv:2009.03514)

Citation key: `james2020artemis`. Original title: "Requirements for gravity measurements on the anticipated Artemis III mission".

## Claim
White paper on the goals and requirements of gravimetry on the lunar surface.

## Numbers (with units and conditions)
Format: value | product | frame realization | statistic type. "not stated" = the source does not say.
- Moon free-air gradient 0.18 mGal/m | model | not applicable | value. NOTE: the candidate list claimed 0.187 mGal/m; the source says 0.18.
- 0.1 mGal reading error over 20 m elevation range -> 159 kg/m³ density error | example.
- 1 m elevation error over 20 m elevation -> ~5 % gravity error, ~100 kg/m³ | example.
- Recommendation: elevation determination better than 1 m, preferably less | requirement; relative vs absolute not stated.

## Height reference
Elevation differences Δh along a survey.

## Application to a walking quadruped on the Moon (LunarLeaper)
Gives a looser height requirement (1 m) than Mittelholz (`mittelholz2026ll`, 0.25 m). The reason: the goal here is bulk density, not detection of voids.
