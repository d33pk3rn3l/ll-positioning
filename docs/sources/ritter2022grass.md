# ritter2022grass: Science goals and status of the GRASS gravimeter on the Hera asteroid mission (Ritter et al., Europlanet Science Congress 2022, abstract 1115)

## Claim
GRAvimeter for small Solar System bodies (GRASS) on Juventas (Hera) will measure the local gravity vector on Dimorphos. Science requirement S#1: gravity vector "with accuracy of <1% in direction and amplitude".

## Numbers (with units and conditions)
Format: value | product | frame realization | statistic type. "not stated" = the source does not say.
- Expected surface gravity on Dimorphos: "around 4.5 x 10^-5 m s^-2 (or 4.5 mGal)" | model | n/a | n/a.
- Requirement S#1: "Determination of local gravity vector at landing location with accuracy of <1% in direction and amplitude" | requirement | n/a | bound. 1% of 4.5 mGal = 0.045 mGal = 45 µGal [derived].

## Application to a walking quadruped on the Moon (LunarLeaper)
The only GRASS accuracy number I found in a GRASS-team text is a 1% requirement at 4.5 mGal (~45 µGal [derived]), which sits inside the team range 20-300 µGal. It is a requirement for Dimorphos, not a measured noise, and not a lunar value. Mittelholz 2026 (batch 1) says "a few tens of microgals" (secondary).
