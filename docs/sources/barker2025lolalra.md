# barker2025lolalra: Laser Ranging from LRO-LOLA to Retro-Reflector Arrays on the Lunar Surface (Barker, Cremons et al., 56th LPSC 2025, abstract 1704)

## Claim
LOLA ranged to the Apollo 11, 14, 15 arrays and to mini-LRAs on Chandrayaan-3, SLIM and Chang'e-6 by slewing LRO.

## Numbers (with units and conditions)
Format: value | product | frame realization | statistic type. "not stated" = the source does not say.
- LRO must point LOLA to the expected LRA position within 0.01° in real time | requirement.
- Apollo LRAs: average measured locations within a few metres cross-track of true; total geolocation precision ~14 m (LRO pointing + spot size) | LOLA | frame not stated | statistic not stated.
- Mini-LRAs: observed location uncertainty "10's of meters peak-to-peak" (receiver FOV ~40 m at 100 km range) | peak-to-peak.
- LRA heights measured by LOLA: several metres peak-to-peak (preliminary orbits) | peak-to-peak.
- Longest ranges 94 km and 116 km; detection rates e.g. 31 % day / 5 % night for CH3 | flight.

## Height reference
Not stated.

## Application to a walking quadruped on the Moon (LunarLeaper)
A mini-LRA on the LunarLeaper lander can be found by LOLA, but present geolocation is ~10s of metres: not better than a NAC tie. Only useful as a long-term fiducial unless orbits improve.
