# kolvenbach2026ll: LunarLeaper - A mission concept to explore the lunar subsurface with a small-scale legged robot (Kolvenbach et al. 2026, Acta Astronautica 240:63-75)

## Claim
Mission concept: a payload-equipped 15 kg-class legged robot approaches the Marius Hills pit within one lunar day, with GPR, gravimeter and camera.

## Application to a walking quadruped on the Moon (LunarLeaper)
Baseline for platform class and payload spacing. Its 1 m gravity spacing conflicts with Mittelholz 2026 (10 m / 5 m). The '~30 kg' claim from the candidate list does not match this paper (15 kg-class). It gives no navigation accuracy.
