# ashby2024clock: A Relativistic Framework to Estimate Clock Rates on the Moon (Ashby & Patla 2024, AJ 168:112) - LOW PRIORITY for nav

## Claim
A clock on the Moon's selenoid runs faster than one on Earth's geoid by about 56.02 µs/day averaged over a lunar orbit.

## Numbers (with units and conditions)
Format: value | product | frame realization | statistic type. "not stated" = the source does not say.
- Lunar surface rate offset: 56.0199 (12) - 0.10843417 cos(f) µs/day (Table 3, eq. 28) | DE440 used for potentials | n/a | value with stated uncertainty (12) in last digits; statistic type not stated.
- Earth satellite at lunar distance (no lunar potential): 58.721 µs/day | illustrative.

## Height reference
Clock on the selenoid (lunar equipotential); not a geometric height.

## Application to a walking quadruped on the Moon (LunarLeaper)
Confirms the ~56 µs/day value. Time scale does not change the body-fixed frame. Low priority for LunarLeaper navigation.
