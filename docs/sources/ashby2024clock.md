# Clock rates on the Moon from relativity (Ashby & Patla 2024, Astronomical Journal 168:112) - LOW PRIORITY for navigation

Citation key: `ashby2024clock`. Original title: "A Relativistic Framework to Estimate Clock Rates on the Moon".

## Claim
A clock on the selenoid of the Moon runs faster than a clock on the geoid of the Earth. The difference is about 56.02 µs/day, averaged over one lunar orbit.

## Numbers (with units and conditions)
Format: value | product | frame realization | statistic type. "not stated" = the source does not say.
- Lunar surface rate offset: 56.0199 (12) - 0.10843417 cos(f) µs/day (Table 3, eq. 28) | DE440 (Development Ephemeris 440) used for potentials | not applicable | value with stated uncertainty (12) in last digits; statistic type not stated.
- Earth satellite at lunar distance (no lunar potential): 58.721 µs/day | illustrative.

## Height reference
Clock on the selenoid (lunar equipotential); not a geometric height.

## Application to a walking quadruped on the Moon (LunarLeaper)
Confirms the ~56 µs/day value. The time scale does not change the body-fixed frame. Low priority for LunarLeaper navigation.
