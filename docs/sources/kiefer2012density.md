# kiefer2012density: The density and porosity of lunar rocks (Kiefer, Macke, Britt, Irving, Consolmagno, Geophysical Research Letters 39:L07201, 2012)
Abbreviations: GRAIL = Gravity Recovery and Interior Laboratory; NWA = Northwest Africa (meteorite name); LAP = LaPaz Icefield; MIL = Miller Range (meteorite names); Ti = titanium; Al = aluminium.

## Claim
Laboratory bulk and grain densities and porosities of 6 Apollo samples and 7 lunar meteorites (bead method for bulk volume, helium pycnometry for grain volume). Mare basalt grain density depends strongly on composition. "Bulk density is important for calculation of gravity anomalies".

## Depth and region
Hand samples from Apollo sites and meteorites of unknown source. Not from Marius Hills. Porosity is micro-crack porosity at hand-sample scale.

## Application to a walking quadruped gravimeter on the Moon near the Marius Hills pit (LunarLeaper)
Gives the measured bulk density range for solid mare basalt (3010-3270 kg/m^3). It applies to intact basalt such as the pit wall and the lava-tube roof, not to the loose regolith at the surface.
