# ashley2011pits: Orbit images show that the Marius Hills pit and other lunar pits open into voids under the surface (Ashley et al., Lunar and Planetary Science Conference 42, 2011, abstract 2771)

## Claim
Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) Narrow Angle Camera (NAC) re-imaging of the Marius Hills pit (incl. a 43° slew oblique view) confirms that the pit floor extends under the mare surface.
